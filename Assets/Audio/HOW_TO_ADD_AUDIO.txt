AUDIO FOLDER STRUCTURE
======================

Drop your .mp3 files into these exact subfolders:

1. Assets/Audio/Gehlee/ (Light Mode)
   - song1.mp3       (Background song 1)
   - song2.mp3       (Background song 2)
   - song3.mp3       (Background song 3)
   - voice1.mp3      (Theme switch voice clip 1 - or gehlee.mp3)
   - voice2.mp3      (Optional theme switch voice clip 2)
   - voice3.mp3      (Optional theme switch voice clip 3)

2. Assets/Audio/Jeo/ (Dark Mode)
   - song1.mp3       (Background song 1)
   - song2.mp3       (Background song 2)
   - song3.mp3       (Background song 3)
   - voice1.mp3      (Theme switch voice clip 1 - or jeo.mp3)
   - voice2.mp3      (Optional theme switch voice clip 2)
   - voice3.mp3      (Optional theme switch voice clip 3)

3. Assets/Audio/SFX/ (Calculator Button Clicks)
   - click.mp3       (Button click sound)
